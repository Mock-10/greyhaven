# 10 — Progression

There are two independent tracks, plus money and property.

## Track A: career rank (one per job, kept when switching)
See 03 for pay, XP and perks. Summary of the insider perks by rank:

| Job | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 | Rank 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Office | — | Tower key card | Armored truck tip-offs | −10% legal purchases |
| Mechanic | −20% repairs and upgrades | Legal respray of hot cars | — | −30% garage add-ons |
| Lumberjack | — | Borrow the log truck | Hidden compartment in the log truck | Remote drops marked |

| Road worker | — | Manhole tunnels | Close a road (ambush) | High-vis disguise: −1★ |
| Farmer | Hear night planes | Airdrop schedule | Barn stash | Farm never searched |
| Scrap yard | Chop shop +15% | Crush hot cars legally | −25% used parts | Chop bay at the yard |

## Track B: criminal reputation
| Tier | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 0 Nobody | Fence (sells basic lockpicks, buys stolen goods), chop shop, dealers (all weapons; prices as in 07), NPC launderer (35%, $1,500/day) |
| 1 Hustler | Tier 1 product supplier · Workbench add-on · pro lockpicks and stethoscope at the fence |
| 2 Known | Airdrop ordering · rival activity begins · better laundering (25%, $3,000/day) · tier 2 product · ATM drill · Counterfeit press · Chop bay · Crew room and hiring · gang-contact bribes |
| 3 Connected | Tier 3 product · Product lab · armored truck intel from contacts · judge bribe · front businesses (laundromat, car wash) · jewellery and docks heists |
| 4 Boss | Nightclub front · bank heist · crew skill training (+10% operation output) |

## Money and property milestones (targets; see 02)
First car → first upgrade → buy a small house → first hideout add-on → first operation → crew → front business → heists.

## End state
There is no hard ending. "Completion" is tracked on a **Life Summary** screen: career ranks, reputation tier, properties, fronts, heists done and a net-worth graph. Optional goals for the 10–15 hour target:
- **Model Citizen:** rank 5 in any job and never jailed.
- **Story chains:** complete every optional mission chain (17).
- **Kingpin:** Boss tier, own the nightclub, complete the bank heist.
- **Double Life:** hold rank 4+ in a job **and** Connected tier at the same time, with no more than one jail sentence.
