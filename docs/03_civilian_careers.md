# 03 — Civilian Careers

## How jobs work
- **Start:** on the first day the player picks one of four starting jobs. All four have similar pay but play very differently.
- **Switching** is free at any time from the job board or a phone call. **Rank is kept separately for every job**, so you can go back to an old job at the rank you left it.
- **Shift:** clock in between 08:00 and 10:00 (late after 09:00: −20% pay for that shift). A shift is **3–4 key tasks**, each one a minigame (see 06), taking about 3–4 real minutes in total. Then clock out and get paid in clean cash.
- **Weekend side gigs** (Saturday and Sunday): optional short gigs at 60% of your rank's pay. They give no promotion XP.
- **Missed shift:** no pay, −5 promotion XP. A bribe to HR can cover it (see 02).

## Performance and promotion
Each task is graded **Poor, OK, Good or Excellent** from the minigame result. Promotion XP per shift is the sum of its tasks:

| Shift result | XP |
|---|---|
| Mostly poor | 6 |
| Mostly OK | 9 |
| Mostly good | 11 |
| Mostly excellent | 14 |

| Rank | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pay per shift (clean) | $180 | $260 | $370 | $510 | $700 |
| XP to reach the next rank | 40 | 70 | 110 | 160 | — |
| One-time promotion bonus | — | $250 | $500 | $900 | $1,500 |

A dedicated player reaches rank 2 around day 4, rank 3 around day 12, rank 4 around day 26 and rank 5 around day 40.

## Effect of an arrest on your career
These apply to **every** job, because the record follows the person:
1. **Promotion freeze** for 3 in-game days: no promotion XP is earned. Lifting it early costs a $600 HR bribe.
2. **Missed shifts while jailed** pay nothing and cost −5 XP each. A $150 HR bribe covers each one.
3. **Extra work for 2 days after release:** each shift has 1 extra task, and the XP requirement for your current rank goes up by +10% until the end of day 2.
4. The weekly **clean-record bonus** (+15% of the week's pay) is lost for that week.

Getting identified on camera but never arrested does **not** affect your job. Only arrests count.

## The jobs
Each job has its own minigames (06), a rank ladder, and **insider perks** that feed into crime. This is how the two lives connect.

### Starting jobs
**Office Worker** — Downtown tower. Ranks: Clerk → Analyst → Senior Analyst → Manager → Director.
- Tasks: *Filing* (sort papers into trays), *Data entry* (type sequence), *Stamp and sign* (timing), *Meeting* (choose dialogue to keep the boss happy; rank 3+).
- Insider perks: rank 2 gets a building **key card** for after-hours access to the tower. Rank 3 gets **tip-offs**: armored truck route times appear on the map. Rank 4 gets **expense account**: −10% on legal purchases.

**Car Mechanic** — garage in Industrial. Ranks: Apprentice → Mechanic → Senior → Shop Lead → Owner's Partner.
- Tasks: *Diagnose* (find the faulty part), *Bolt torque* (timing), *Tyre change* (sequence), *Respray* (even sweep).
- Insider perks: rank 1 gets **−20% repairs and upgrades**. Rank 2 lets you **respray stolen cars legally** for $400 (clears "hot" status). Rank 4 makes hideout **garage add-ons 30% cheaper**.

**Lumberjack** — lumber camp in the Outskirts. Ranks: Hand → Feller → Rigger → Crew Boss → Camp Boss.
- Tasks: *Chop* (swing timing), *Fell direction* (aim), *Load logs* (stacking), *Haul* (drive the log truck to the mill).
- Insider perks: rank 2 gets a **truck license** to borrow the log truck. Rank 3 unlocks **hidden compartments**: shipments carried in the log truck lose 50% less value from damage and pass police stops. Rank 4 marks **remote drops** in the forest on the map.

**Delivery Driver** — depot in Suburbs. Ranks: Courier → Driver → Senior Driver → Route Planner → Depot Manager.
- Tasks: *Load van* (stacking), *Route* (pick the shortest of 3 routes), *Doorstep* (timed hand-off, finding the right door), *Signature* (trace).
- Insider perks: rank 1 gives a **uniform** that makes witnesses 30% slower to call in your daytime crimes in Suburbs. Rank 2 gives **route knowledge**: +20% time on smuggling deliveries. Rank 3 lets you see **house occupancy**, which shows the best burglary targets.

### Later jobs (unlocked from the job board after day 5)
You are **never fired** from any job, however many arrests you have (decided). Arrests only cause the effects listed above.

**Security Guard** — banks and stores in Downtown. Ranks: Guard → Senior Guard → Supervisor → Site Lead → Head of Security.
- Tasks: *Patrol* (reach checkpoints in time), *Monitor cameras* (spot the shoplifter), *ID check* (spot the fake).
- Insider perks: rank 2 shows **camera blind spots** as a faint overlay. Rank 3 can **disable cameras** in stores for 60 seconds. Rank 4 shows **vault schedules**, which make heist prep shorter.

**Road Worker** — city crews, all districts. Ranks: Labourer → Operator → Foreman → Supervisor → Site Manager.
- Tasks: *Paint lines* (steady sweep), *Jackhammer* (rhythm), *Traffic control* (stop/go timing), *Cone layout* (placement).
- Insider perks: rank 2 gives **maintenance tunnels**: manhole shortcuts that lose pursuers (break line of sight). Rank 3 lets you **close a road** to set up an armored truck ambush. Rank 4 gives a **high-vis disguise**: −1 star at the moment you put it on (once per chase).

**Farmer** — Old Pete's farm, Outskirts. Ranks: Farmhand → Tractor Driver → Field Hand Lead → Foreman → Farm Partner.
- Tasks: *Plough / plant* (drive the tractor along furrow lines), *Harvest* (timing), *Sort produce* (sorting), *Irrigate* (open valves in sequence).
- Insider perks: rank 1 means you **hear the night planes**: a radar ping when an airdrop lands in a nearby field. Rank 2 gives the **airdrop schedule**: drop times and fields are marked a day ahead. Rank 3 gives a **barn stash** on the farm, a safe place to hide airdrop crates and product. Rank 4 gives **field access**: police never search the farm grounds.
- Design intent (the user's idea): working the farm teaches the player that product arrives through the fields, so the honest job naturally reveals the airdrop crime route.

**Scrap Yard Worker** — legal scrap yard, Industrial. Ranks: Sorter → Crane Operator → Crusher Operator → Yard Lead → Yard Manager.
- Tasks: *Crane magnet* (position and drop), *Crush* (timing), *Sort metal* (sorting by type), *Weigh-in* (read the scale and enter the value).
- Insider perks: rank 1 lets you **hear about chop shop deals**: the chop shop pays +15%. Rank 2 lets you **crush a hot car legally**: it is destroyed, its "hot" status is cleared, and if unseen it clears 1★ (once per chase). Rank 3 gives **−25% on used parts** for upgrades. Rank 4 lets you run the **chop bay at the yard** without owning a hideout add-on.


## Decisions (question round of 2026-10-11, before autopilot)
- **Weekend side gigs** are separate odd jobs open to everyone: car wash and a bar shift at The Low Tide (see 02).
- **Work uniforms** come off only when you change at a wardrobe or a work locker. Forgetting is the risk (100% identification during crimes, docs/04).
- **Mechanic garage (Torque & Sons):** one bay with a lift and a toolbench, a spray booth, a small office to clock in. 08:00–18:00, 1–2 coworkers.
- **Lumber camp (Ashby Timber):** outdoors (trees, loading yard, the log truck to the mill), plus a site trailer to clock in.
- **Delivery (Swiftish Couriers):** a depot interior (loading bay and counter), then drive the van to marked doors on a short row of houses.
- **Office Meeting task:** Claude writes the dialogue in the dry office tone; the user reviews it afterwards.

## Phase 2 build (milestone 5, 2026-10-11)
- **Missed shift:** any workday with no shift worked or started by 10:00 (except day 1, the guided first day) costs 5 XP, never below 0. HR can cover it from the phone (CONTACTS) within 7 days.
- **Arrest effects** apply to every job: a 3-day promotion freeze from the arrest (XP earned meanwhile is lost), extra work until the end of the second day after release (one more task per shift and a 10% higher XP bar), no clean-record bonus that week, and a shift in progress is lost. The arrest screen says so.
- **Pay goes to the bank** ("+$180 TO THE BANK"), and clocking out autosaves.
- **Job board** on the phone: switch jobs any time off shift; rank is kept per job. The garage, the lumber camp and the depot show NOT HIRING YET until their milestone (M7).

## Phase 2 build (milestone 7, 2026-10-12)
- **All four jobs are hiring.** Switch on the phone's job board (off shift). Each job has its own site with a time clock, a boss who speaks the toasts, and a uniform from the site's locker:

  | Job | Site, clock in with | Tasks (shift) | Uniform |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Office | Pellridge Tower floor 4, Ms. Hollis | filing, data entry, stamp and sign (the copier), meeting (the whiteboard, rank 3+); 3 a shift | none |
  | Mechanic | Torque & Sons on Hedgerow Way, the shop foreman | diagnose, torque bolts, change tyre, respray; 3 a shift | blue-grey coveralls |
  | Lumberjack | Ashby Timber's site office east of Ashby Road, the site boss | chop (the block by the stump), fell trees (the yard's north edge), load logs (the log pile); 3 a shift, outdoors | red flannel |
  | Delivery | Swiftish Couriers on Linden Avenue, dispatch | load van and plan route at the depot, then deliver parcels and get a signature at two marked stops on Linden Avenue; 4 a shift, each once, in that order | navy and khaki |
- **Clocking in** only works at your current job's time clock ("YOU DON'T WORK HERE" elsewhere), and jobs with a uniform need it on first. Clock out where you clocked in. The pay, XP, late and arrest rules above are the same for every job.
- **Tasks** can repeat within a shift (a mechanic can do two resprays), except the delivery steps. The office meeting needs rank 3; below that its prompt reads MEETING (RANK 3+).
- **Uniforms** come from the site's locker and come off at any locker or wardrobe (docs/03 decisions). After clocking out in uniform the objective reminds you to change. The name tag (100% identification in crimes, docs/04) arrives with identification in Phase 3.
- **The radar** marks the job in gold: the site's door when it's time to clock in, the yard spots on a lumber shift, and the two delivery stops.
- **Perks** (phone, JOB BOARD, PERKS): the office key card (rank 2) and the **expense account** (office rank 4: 10% off in every legal shop, clothes and cars) work now. The others show as LATER until their systems exist: armored truck tip-offs (Phase 3/7), repairs and garage add-ons (Phases 4–5), respraying stolen cars (Phase 4), the log truck licence and compartments and remote drops (Phase 5), the courier uniform's witness delay, route knowledge and house occupancy (Phase 3/5).
- **Haul** (driving the log truck to the mill) waits for the mill in Phase 6; a lumber shift is chop, fell and load.
- **Boss names** are roles (shop foreman, site boss, dispatch) until the user names them.

## Phase 2 build (milestone 8, 2026-10-12)
- **Weekend odd jobs** are built: the car wash at Squeaky's (east of Torque & Sons, E at the wash apron) and the bar shift at The Low Tide (E at the beer taps). Both are listed on the phone's JOB BOARD with their hours and whether they are open now. A gig is three minigame tasks (scrub a car's side; pour drinks for a counter rush). It pays 60% of the current job's shift pay plus tips (docs/02) and gives no promotion XP. You can't start a gig while on shift, and Esc in a task walks off the gig unpaid.
