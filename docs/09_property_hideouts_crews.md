Every home is a **save point**, a **sleep point** and has a **stash** (safe storage for dirty cash and items).

## Hideout add-ons
A hideout is not a separate building. It is an **add-on to a property you own**. Each add-on uses one slot.

| Add-on | Rep tier | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stash room | 0 | $3,000 | +20 stash slots; police raids can't find it |
| Garage bay | 0 | $6,000 | +2 parking (mechanic rank 4: −30%) |
| Workbench | 1 | $4,000 | Craft and repair tools; respray at home |
| Counterfeit press | 2 | $15,000 | Printing operation |
| Chop bay | 2 | $20,000 | Chop shop operation |
| Product lab | 3 | $30,000 | Lab operation |
| Crew room | 2 | $8,000 | Required to hire crew (cap 4) |

## Operations (passive income that needs attention)
Operations **only run while stocked**. When supplies run out, they **stall** and show a red icon on the map. The player restocks in person, or an operator crew member restocks automatically (paying with dirty cash from the stash).

| Operation | Input | Cycle | Output | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Counterfeit press | $1,000 dirty supplies | 2 days | $2,600 fake cash (counts as dirty) | Money-count minigame on collection gives a bonus |
| Chop bay | 1 stolen car | 1 day | 1.5× the chop-shop price, as dirty money | Needs a car delivered |
| Product lab | $3,000 dirty supplies | 2 days | Tier 2 product worth $7,500 at the drop | Must still be smuggled (or sent with a crew driver) |

**Rival hits:** see 04 (light rivals). Operations can be hit by rival crews; you get a 1-hour warning to defend.

**Raid risk:** every night an operation runs while you are a "known face" or above 3★, there is a 5% police raid chance. A raid seizes that operation's current batch. The stash room protects the stash.

## Crews
- **Cap of 4** members. Needs the Crew room add-on. Hire at the bar (Downtown) from rep tier 2.
- Hire fee $1,000–3,000 (dirty). Weekly wage $300–700 (dirty, paid from the stash automatically). Unpaid crew quit after 3 days.

| Role | What they do | Stats |
|---|---|---|
| **Operator** | Assigned to one operation. Restocks it automatically and keeps it running. Takes 15% of output. | Skill: +0–20% output |
| **Driver** | Runs a smuggling shipment by themselves, or a shipment from your lab | Reliability: cargo loss 10–25% by tier; 5% chance they get caught (cargo lost, driver jailed 2 days) |

Crew members have a name, a portrait (generated from the character art) and 1–2 stats. They are not physically present in the world, except as a driver's car on the road during a delivery.

## Front businesses
See 02 for laundering numbers. Fronts are bought properties with their own building sprite. They launder automatically from the stash each night and earn clean revenue. They are a late-game goal for both criminal and mixed players.

## Phase 5 build (milestone 15, 2026-10-12): homes, the stash, the hideout
- **Two houses for sale on Linden Avenue** (Hedgerow Park, the suburbs): **4 Linden Avenue** (small house, $12,000) and **10 Linden Avenue** (family house, $30,000), each with a FOR SALE sign on the lawn. E on the sign shows the house and BUY (clean money: the bank first, then carried cash). The sign goes; the front door becomes ENTER HOME, and the house is no longer a burglary target (deliveries still come).
- **A home** is the bungalow or family layout lived in by you: the bed sleeps and saves (a save point even while the apartment is lost), the wardrobe changes outfits, a loose floorboard is the **stash**, and a door in the bedroom leads down to **the basement**. Walking in unseen with 1–2★ clears them (docs/05).
- **The stash:** dirty cash (no slots) and weapons and goods, one slot each; 10 slots, +20 with the stash room. The rented apartment's stash works the same way.
- **The basement hideout** (one big concrete space): the stairs up, a lounge corner, the roll door up to the driveway, and the **plans table**, where add-ons are bought with clean money. Bought add-ons appear as stations: the **stash room** (its locked door opens, a safe is the stash), the **workbench** (lockpicks, repair kits and fuel cans at half the store's price; respray the car on the driveway for $400, same as the garage). The garage bay adds 2 to the garage (−30% for a mechanic of rank 4+). The press, chop bay and lab come in M16, the crew room in M17; the plans list them as "coming soon".
- **The garage is the driveway:** E GARAGE at the end of it stores the car standing there (up to the home's parking: 1 small, 2 family, +2 with the bay) or brings a stored car back out. Stored cars keep their health, fuel and trunk. After loading a save, your last car waits on your driveway.
- **Tax** is taken with Monday's rent from the bank; what the bank can't cover is owed and taken the next Monday (assumed: no foreclosure).
- **Assumptions:** the two houses on the market and their numbers (4 and 10); the workbench crafts at half price with clean money; the garage is storage at the driveway rather than extra physical spots; industrial units and the waterfront warehouse come with their districts (Phase 6).

## Phase 5 build (milestone 16, 2026-10-12): the press and the chop bay
- **Counterfeit press** (rep tier 2, $15,000): a station by the stash room. RESTOCK THE PRESS pays $1,000 in dirty cash (carried first, then the home's stash) and starts a 2-day run worth $2,600. When it's done the press **stalls**: a phone message, and the home's radar marker turns red until it's restocked. COLLECT opens the **money count** (docs/06): the grade adds a bonus to the fake notes (excellent +15%, good +10%, ok +5%; assumed), paid as dirty cash.
- **Chop bay** (rep tier 2, $20,000): a lift on the marked floor below the roll door. Drive a stolen car (any car you don't own) onto the driveway, then at the chop bay CHOP THE CAR: the car is gone, and a day later 1.5× what the chop shop would have paid waits as dirty cash.
- **Raids:** each night at 03:00 (assumed hour), while you're a known face or above 3★, every running operation has a 5% chance of a police raid. It seizes the running batch and, unless the home has a stash room, the stash's dirty cash and goods.
- **Still to come (M17):** the product lab (its product needs smuggling to sell), operators who restock automatically, and rival hits.
